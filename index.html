About this app

Direct Line connects two phones to each other instead of routing through a company's servers. With internet, it opens a direct link for text, voice and video. Without internet, it hops from phone to phone over Bluetooth until the message arrives.

End-to-end encrypted Relays carry it, they can't read it.
Voice and video calls Direct connection when you're online.
Bluetooth mesh Every phone extends the range.
Messages wait Queued until a path opens up.

How it works

01
Find

Open Nearby. Phones running Direct Line show up. Scan a QR code once to save someone.

02
Lock

Both phones swap keys and seal the line before anything is sent.

03
Carry

Too far to reach directly? The message hops through phones in between until it lands.
