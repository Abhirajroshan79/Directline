Hey, I'm **Abhishek Kumar Paswan**, a B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at Government Engineering College Ambikapur. 

Between attending classes and preparing for my university exams, I spent countless late nights building Direct Line. I wanted to create a communication tool that is completely secure, truly private, and doesn't rely on big corporate servers to keep us connected. 

Whether you're off the grid, at a crowded festival, or dealing with a power cut, Direct Line keeps you talking.

## ✨ Why Direct Line?

*   🔒 **End-to-End Encrypted & Private:** Your conversations are for your eyes only. Nothing is stored anywhere, on any server.
*   👻 **Completely Anonymous:** No phone number, email, or account required. Just pick a name and connect.
*   🌐 **Works Online & Offline:** 
    *   *Online:* Establishes a seamless peer-to-peer (P2P) connection for secure text, voice, and video.
    *   *Offline:* Automatically switches to a Bluetooth mesh network. Your message hops securely from phone to phone until it reaches its destination.
*   🛡️ **Safe & Secure:** Other phones can relay your offline messages through the mesh, but because of the encryption, they can never read them.
