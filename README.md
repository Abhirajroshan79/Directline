<div align="center">
  <h1>Direct Line</h1>
  <p>Messaging and calling that works even when the internet goes down.</p>
</div>

You don't need to create an account, share your email, or give out your phone number. Just pick a name and start chatting. 

### How it works
When you have a signal, the app connects peer-to-peer for private calls and texts. If you lose your connection, it switches to a Bluetooth mesh network. Your messages hop from phone to phone until they reach the right person. 

Everything you send is end-to-end encrypted. The phones relaying your offline messages can't read them, and nothing is ever stored on a central server. 

### Support future updates
The app is totally free, has no ads, and tracks nothing. 

Writing code for new features and fixing bugs takes up a lot of my free time. If Direct Line helps you stay connected when you need it most, please consider sending a small tip. Your donations keep the project alive and help me push the next update.
