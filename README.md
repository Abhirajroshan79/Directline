# Direct Line

Messaging and calling that actually works when you don't have internet.

I built Direct Line because we've all been in situations where the network completely drops—whether it's a crowded festival, a dead spot in a campus basement, or during a power cut. Instead of relying on a company's server, this app connects phones directly to each other. 

If your friend is too far away to reach directly, the app uses a Bluetooth mesh network. Your message will securely "hop" through other phones in the area until it reaches its destination.

## What it does
* **No internet needed:** Bounces messages from phone to phone using a Bluetooth mesh.
* **Works normally online:** If you have data, it opens a direct peer-to-peer connection for voice, video, and text.
* **No accounts:** You don't need to hand over your phone number or email. Just pick a name and scan a friend's QR code to connect.
* **Actually private:** End-to-end encrypted. The phones carrying your offline messages cannot read them.

## How to get it
The app is 100% free, has zero ads, and doesn't track you. You can download the latest version directly from the [website](https://abhirajroshan79.github.io/Directline/).

1. Download the `.apk` file.
2. If prompted, tell your browser to "Download anyway".
3. Allow "Install from unknown sources" on your Android phone and install.
4. Open the app, grant Bluetooth/Nearby device permissions, and you're good to go.
